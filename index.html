BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A few things to know.

Everything you need to feel comfortable before you enquire.

How early should I book?

We recommend enquiring a few days ahead, especially for weekends. Your preferred slot is confirmed directly by the studio.

Can I book a home visit?

Home visits may be available within the studio's service area. Share your preferred location when enquiring to confirm availability.

How will I know the final price?

The displayed prices are illustrative. The studio will confirm the final cost and availability before your appointment is booked.

What if I need to reschedule?

Contact the studio on WhatsApp as early as possible. The rescheduling policy and alternative slots can be confirmed directly.

What about sensitive skin or allergies?

Mention any sensitivities or allergies before booking so the provider can discuss appropriate products and precautions with you.
